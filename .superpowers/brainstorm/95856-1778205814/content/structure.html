Landing page — section flow

Same structure across all 3 themes. Scroll-order from top to bottom.

1. Header / Nav

Sticky. Logo, nav links (Features, Pricing, About), dual CTA buttons. Minimal, no dropdowns.

2. Hero — "Run your entire business from one place"

Headline + subtext + dual CTA (Start free trial / Book demo). Right side: interactive dashboard mockup or animated terminal showing Letis in action. This is the make-or-break section — animation and typography must be exceptional.

3. Trust bar

"Everything you need to run a modern retail business" — feature count badges: 20+ Modules, POS Terminal, Inventory, Accounting, AI Reports, HRM. No fake logos — real capability statements.

4. Core modules grid — "Everything included"

6–8 cards in an asymmetric or staggered grid. Point of Sale, Inventory Management, Accounting, Purchases & Suppliers, Reports & Analytics, AI Insights, HRM & Payroll, Multi-store. Each card: icon, title, one-line description. Visual differentiation between cards (not uniform).

5. "How it works" — 3 steps

1. Create your account (30s) → 2. Add your products → 3. Start selling. Timeline or connected cards. Simple, fast, no fluff.

6. Feature highlight — "AI That Works For You"

Deeper dive into the AI capabilities. Smart inventory predictions, sales forecasting, automated reports. Split layout: text left, visual/illustration right. This is a differentiator — most POS systems don't have AI.

7. Testimonials / Social Proof

Carousel or masonry grid of quotes. Real names, roles, business types. Focus on outcomes: "saved 10hrs/week", "grew revenue 30%".

8. Pricing — 3 tiers

Starter (free / low cost), Professional, Enterprise. Clear feature comparison. Annual/monthly toggle. Starter tier has a prominent "Start free" CTA.

9. FAQ

Accordion. 5–6 common questions covering offline use, hardware compatibility, data migration, support, security.

10. Final CTA — "Ready to run smarter?"

Full-width, bold. Big headline, dual CTA buttons, no distractions. This closes the page.

11. Footer

Logo, copyright, social links, stacked nav (Products, Company, Legal). Clean and simple.

Page length

  • 11 sections is substantial but not bloated
  • Each section serves a distinct purpose
  • Scroll-driven narrative arc: problem → solution → proof → action

Risks

  • Must balance feature density with scannability
  • AI section needs real capability, not hype
  • Pricing tiers need thought — don't want decision paralysis
