Which A4 documents should we tackle first?

The legacy system generated all of these. Which are highest priority for the new system?

Q

Quotations / Estimates

Professional A4 quotes sent to customers before a sale — branding, line items, terms, validity period, signature area

P

Purchase Orders

A4 POs sent to suppliers — items to order, delivery address, payment terms, approval block

R

Payment Receipts

A4 payment acknowledgements — amount paid, method, balance due, official stamp area

X

Sales & Purchase Returns

A4 return authorizations — returned items, reason codes, refund/credit details

S

Stock Documents

Adjustments, transfers, stock counts — warehouse records, batch tracking, approval signatures

D

Delivery Notes / Packing Slips

A4 shipping documents — items dispatched, quantities, carrier info, recipient signature
