What it takes
Per template: 1 .hbs file (~150 lines HTML/CSS) + 2 lines in DocumentService.TEMPLATE_FILES + 1 line in TemplateService.BUILT_IN_TYPES
Frontend: Add DocumentActionsBar to the corresponding pages (same pattern as Phase 1)
Total effort: ~3 days for all 25 templates
