Naming Proposal

A

"Intelligence"

B

"Insights"

Reports → Insights Hub. AI → Smart Insights. More common in SaaS but also less distinctive. Feels like a dashboard widget rather than a strategic tool.

C

"Letis Cortex"

Reports → Cortex (the thinking layer of your business). Bold, memorable, brandable. Risk: might feel gimmicky if the AI isn't powerful enough yet.
